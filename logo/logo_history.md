# Logo history

The research behind the timestamps in the logo file names, one section per
company in alphabetical order. Each item is a moment at which a logo took
effect, in UTC, in the format

    - `YYYY-MM-DDThh:mm:ss` — what the logo became
      + precision: day | month | year | decade — what the timestamp is taken from
      + source: <url>
      + file: <name in this directory, or "missing">

A photo is framed with the logo in effect when it was taken, and one older than
every logo of its company falls back to `default.svg`. Every change wants a
file, including those from before digital photography: a photograph on film can
be scanned and given the date it was taken, so a mark of 1960 can still be the
right one to frame. Where a file is missing, the reason is recorded beside it.
Where a company has a single logo here and its adoption date is not documented,
the timestamp is a lower bound, marked as such, so that photos of that make keep
their logo instead of falling back.

## meizu

- `2003-01-01T00:00:00` — a circle enclosing an "M", after the carved seals of

Three marks in the first twelve years, then two more. The seal script of 2010
never replaced the wordmark: ifanr, writing when the mark of 2015 appeared, has
it that 「最早在 M8 和 M9 的时候，我们还可以在魅族手机上看到有篆体印章风格的魅族
标志，到后来，就成了现在机身背后的 MEIZU 字母」, the seal riding on the back of
the M8 and the M9 and giving way to the letters on later bodies, while every
account of 2015 has the brand itself giving up the wordmark that year. A drawing
of the seal is published, at https://logos-download.com/brands/meizu, but it is
deliberately not installed: a frame carries the mark of the company rather than
the badge on the back of the camera, and a file for the seal would take every
photograph from 2010 to 2015 away from the wordmark, which is the mark the brand
was using throughout.

The mark of 2003 needs no file either, the first Meizu camera being the M8 of
2009.

The drawing for 2004 is a revision that `File:OPPO Logo.svg` on Commons has

`File:Oppo logo.gif`, uploaded to Commons in July 2008, and the 2004 image on
1000logos. Both are black, and both cover more of it than of the drawing of 2013
(86% and 94% of their union against 77% and 74%). The change of 2013 is one of
weight rather than of shape: at equal height the letters grew 6% wider, with
thicker ellipses, smaller counters and tighter spacing.

A file dated 2004 on logos-download.com draws instead a bold serif oppo. No
account of the brand mentions a serif mark, the file carries no source, and the
two references above rule it out for 2008 at least, so it is not used here.
